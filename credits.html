Demo details.

Form is a product of NAADRA LLC, Wyoming, United States. This read-only demonstration uses a fictional Terra Atelier catalog, fictional prices and locally authored compositions. It does not establish live model generation quality or independently verified product facts.

What the controls do

Direction buttons select separate responsive storefronts built with Form’s existing shared design validator and renderer. Revision and Undo switch between static, previously accepted fictional QA versions. They make no model requests. The build verified exact Undo and persisted history in isolated temporary storage; your browser does not save a project.

The Shopify sample ZIP matches the selected original, v1, or recorded revision, v2, in the selected direction. It contains native homepage, collection, product and cart templates. All local product references are unbound. Native merchant product selection is required after installation. The local preview has no connected checkout or cart. No Shopify changes occur here.

Data in this demo

No account, form submission, upload, analytics script, tracking cookie or browser storage is used. The demo application retains no visitor requests, IP addresses or identifying request logs. Network and hosting providers still process connection metadata to serve the page and protect their services. No visitor input is sent to OpenAI.
